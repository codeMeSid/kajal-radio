# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Friends and family opening a personal station URL on a phone, usually at night, one-handed. Sid is the operator: add a person, a scene, a short mix.

## Product Purpose

A house of small radio pages. Each slug is one person, one illustrated room, one YouTube-backed mix. Success is they open the link, hit play, and stay inside the scene. The player is furniture in the room, not a music-app chrome.

## Brand Personality

Intimate, lamp-lit, cinematic. Hindi-film interiors and Devanagari wordmarks. Brass light on dark rooms. Quiet enough that the painting does the talking.

## Anti-references

Spotify / Apple Music mini-players. SoundCloud embeds. Generic frosted-glass capsules that clip on phones. SaaS card grids. Editorial magazine layouts (serif drop caps, numbered section kickers).

## Design Principles

- The scene is the product. UI sits on it; it never competes.
- One station, one room, one mix. No library, no search, no account.
- Phone first. Thumbs reach the transport. Titles must be readable, never squeezed by empty columns.
- Vinyl is the album and the video. Expanding it is watching, not navigating away.
- Identity lives in the rooms and the wordmarks, not in player chrome.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast for player text and controls. 44px minimum touch targets on coarse pointers. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` (vinyl spin already does). `viewport-fit: cover` with safe-area insets. Keyboard: seek arrows, Escape closes the expanded video.
